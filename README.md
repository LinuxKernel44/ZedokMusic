# ZedokMusic

A digital audio workstation for Android that runs **real Windows VST3 plugins**.

> **Status: alpha (0.8.0).** It works end to end on a Galaxy S10 (Android 12), but it is young. Expect rough edges — see *Known limitations*.

## What it does

- Multitrack arrangement, piano roll, mixer (gain, pan, mute/solo, level meters), tempo and looping, on-screen keyboard.
- **Zedok native plugins** (no Windows runtime involved, they run inside the audio engine): **Zedok Hybrid** (analog, wavetable and FM synthesizer with unison, two filters, three envelopes, three LFOs, a modulation matrix, an arpeggiator and built-in chorus, delay and reverb), **Zedok Grain** (granular synthesizer: your own samples or built-in textures, scrub and freeze, polyphonic or mono/legato), **Zedok Sampler** (single-sample instrument: root note, start/end, forward / ping-pong / sustain loops with crossfade, reverse, fades, normalize, chops from 2 to 32 slices on the keys, Resample or Stretch time handling with sample BPM and tempo sync, volume / filter / pitch envelopes with delay-attack-hold-decay-sustain-release, LFO to pitch, volume, pan and cutoff, resonant filter, glide, mono / legato, cut itself, one-shot; drop a sound from the browser on its track to load it), **Zedok Piano** (a sampled concert grand: Salamander Grand Piano V3, 16 velocity layers cross-faded, damper and key-off noise, sustain pedal and una corda as automatable parameters with half-pedal, sympathetic string resonance, tuning with A4 415-466, five historical temperaments and piano stretch, humanize, tone, lid, width and reverb), **Zedok Drums** (16-pad synthesized drum machine), **Zedok EQ + Comp** (6-band equalizer with live spectrum, compressor and brickwall limiter), **Zedok Space** (reverb and tempo-synced delay) and **Zedok Color** (chorus, ensemble, flanger, phaser, saturation, bit crusher). They have their own touch screens and about 425 factory presets in a browser with categories, search, favourites, previous/next, A/B, your own presets (saved in the ZedokMusic folder, shared by all projects) and import/export. **Sample drum kits** in Zedok Drums: import a kit folder (one-shot pack, multikit or SFZ) with the Android folder picker; the files the kit uses are copied into the project's own Kits folder. A sound browser (preview, favourites, search) loads single sounds onto pads. See `docs/native-plugins.md`.
- **VST2 plugins (64-bit `.dll`)** are hosted next to VST3: they are found in `Plugins/VST2`, `C:\Program Files\VSTPlugins` (where most VST2 installers put them), `Steinberg\VstPlugins`, and in the phone storage in folders with "vst" in their path; parameters, MIDI, automation, the plugin's own editor and the desktop licence/setup window work as for VST3. A `.dll` is checked once; files that are not VST2 plugins are ignored.
- **Init preset**: the first entry of every preset browser (built-in plugins and the Windows plugins' presets list): every control back to its default and everything loaded removed (samples, kit). If you changed the current sound, a clear message asks first.
- **Project scale**: the button in the piano roll header (e.g. "C major") sets the project's root and scale (major, minor, modes, pentatonics, blues...); rows in the scale are lighter, the others darker, the root is tinted; saved with the project.
- **Note names everywhere**: every key of the on-screen keyboard and of the piano roll shows its name with the octave (C, C#, D ... B), and so does every note in the piano roll.
- **Block screenshots** (Settings → Display, off by default): the phone's screenshot gestures cannot act in the app (uses the secure-window flag, which also blocks other screenshots and recordings; debug builds ignore it).
- **Lit keys**: on the on-screen keyboard and the piano roll's keys, a key takes the track's colour while it sounds: touched with a finger, played from a MIDI keyboard, previewed, or played by the notes of the track at the playhead.
- **Rounded screen corners**: the app keeps a side margin so buttons are not cut by the rounded corners of the screen; automatic from the phone's corner radius, or None / Small / Medium / Large in Settings → Display.
- **On-screen keyboard size**: drag the handle in the middle of the Keys bar up or down; the height is remembered.
- **Hybrid preset preview**: choosing a preset in Zedok Hybrid (list, ◀ ▶) plays C4 for a moment so you hear it at once; Settings → Plugins lets you pick C5 or turn it off.
- **Piano roll selection (like FL Studio)**: rectangle selection (long press in empty space then drag, or the **Select** button), tap to add or remove notes in Select mode, drag a selected note to move the whole selection, drag a right end to stretch all selected notes. A floating bar over the selection offers **Clone** (like Ctrl+B: copies after the selection and selects the copies), Copy, Cut and Delete; a row at the bottom offers transpose (±1, ±12), velocity, Quantize, length, Select all and Invert; **Paste** appears at the playhead when nothing is selected. ↶ ↷ undo and redo the notes (50 steps per track).
- **Audio clips**: every clip of an audio track shows its real waveform (computed once, cached in the project's `Cache/Peaks`). Long press a clip: its full name, **Edit clip…** and **Remove clip**. The editor sets, per clip, gain (heard at once), pitch (semitones, with decimals), speed (with the sample's BPM and *Fit to project BPM*) and the method: *Stretch* (time and pitch independent) or *Resample* (speed and pitch together). Pitch and speed render a processed copy in `Samples/.processed`; the original is kept and *Reset* returns to it.
- **Clip selection** (like the piano roll): tap a clip, rectangle with a long press in empty space or with the **Sel** tool, tap to add or remove in Sel mode, drag one selected clip to move all of them; the bottom bar offers Clone, Copy, Cut, Del, Edit (edits all selected clips), All and Paste at the playhead.
- **No clicks when scrubbing**: when the playhead jumps during playback (dragging the seek bar, tapping the ruler, stopping), the step between the last sample and the new signal now decays over 10 ms and the sound fades in over 8 ms after the silent pre-roll.
- **Project scale dialog** scrolls as one piece (roots, then every mode), so the list of modes is no longer squeezed into one cut-off line on a landscape phone.
- **Rack and Pattern mode** (0.55.0): the new **Rack** tab is a step sequencer for the current pattern: a grid of sixteenth-note steps (the length of the pattern, scrollable), one row per instrument track, plus the 16 pads of a Zedok Drums track as rows of their own. Tap a step to switch a note on or off (it sounds once), drag across steps to paint or erase (the first step you touch decides which), change a melodic track's step pitch with its − / + (default C4). The row of pattern chips on top switches or creates patterns, and **Play: Song / Pattern** chooses what the transport plays: **Song** plays the playlist; **Pattern** loops the current pattern alone from its start (the audio clips are silent in that mode), as in FL Studio. The same switch is in the PATTERNS menu of the Tracks view (a warning shows in the lane header while Pattern mode is on).
- **Patterns and a playlist** (0.54.0): the notes of your tracks now live in **patterns** (a block of notes for every instrument track), and a **pattern lane** at the top of the Tracks view places them in the song. Tap an empty place of the lane to put the current pattern there (on the beat, on the row you tapped); drag a placement to move it (or to another row); drag its dark right end to repeat the pattern (longer) or cut it (shorter); tap it to edit its pattern; long press for **Edit pattern**, **Make unique (own copy)**, **Length** (1 / 2 / 4 / 8 bars), **Clone after it** and **Delete**. Every placement of a pattern plays the same notes: edit the pattern in the piano roll and all of them change. The **PATTERNS** header (left of the lane) shows the current pattern and a menu: switch pattern, **New pattern**, **Duplicate**, **Rename…**, **Length** (automatic = the last note rounded up to whole bars, or 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 bars) and **Delete pattern**. Tapping a track's row inside a placement opens the piano roll on that placement's pattern, and the piano roll's play cursor shows the position inside the pattern while one of its placements plays. Track rows draw all the notes the playlist puts on them. Everything is undoable. Saved with the project (project format 4: a `patterns` list, a `playlist` and the `currentPattern`; an older project becomes "Pattern 1" placed once at the start, and plays exactly as before). The maths is in `Patterns.kt` (7 unit tests).
- **Slide notes** (0.53.0): select notes and press **Slide** in the selection bar (a dark arrow marks a slide note). A slide note does not start a new voice: it glides from the note that is still sounding (the latest note that covers its start, or ends exactly there) to its own pitch, in the instrument's Glide time (at least 70 ms), with no new attack; the note it slides from is not released at its own end. Works in Zedok Hybrid and Zedok Sampler; other instruments and Windows plugins play a slide note as an ordinary note. Saved with the project (a tenth number in the note's entry).
- **Piano roll note tools** (0.52.0), in the new **Tools** menu of the piano roll header: **Ghost notes** (the notes of the other instrument tracks drawn faintly behind the grid), **Paint notes** (drag in the grid to lay a run of notes, one per note length, following the finger's row; one undo step), **Chord stamp…** (pick a chord type and an inversion, then each tap in the grid places the chord with the tapped row as its root; the chord button in the header switches it off), and the dialogs **Quantize…** (grid, starts / ends / both, strength, swing, window), **Humanize…** (timing, velocity and length randomness, repeatable seed), **Strum…** (spread a chord over time, lowest- or highest-first, tension), **Arpeggiate…** (up, down, up-down, random, as played; rate, octaves, gate), **Riff machine…** (a phrase in the project scale: length, rate, range, density, leaps, seed, optionally replacing the bars) and **Event LFO…** (draws a sine / triangle / ramp / square / random wave on velocity, pan, cutoff, resonance, fine pitch or release across the notes). They work on the selected notes, or on every note of the track when none is selected, and each is one undo step. The selection bar also names the chord the selected notes form ("3 notes · Cmaj7", with inversions as "C/E"). The maths is in `NoteTools.kt` with 14 unit tests.
- **Per-note properties** (0.51.0): like FL Studio, every note can carry its own **pan**, **cutoff**, **resonance**, **fine pitch** (±100 cents) and **release** (time ×¼ … ×4). The lane under the piano roll has a selector at its left ("Velocity ▾"): pick Pan, Cutoff, Resonance, Fine pitch or Release and drag the stems as for velocity (the middle line means unchanged). Zedok Hybrid uses all five (cutoff ±3 octaves, resonance ±50 %), Zedok Sampler all five, Zedok Drums pan, fine pitch (±1 semitone), cutoff (as the pad's tone) and release (as the decay length), Zedok Piano pan, fine pitch and release; Zedok Grain and the Windows plugins ignore them. Saved with the project (project format 3: the five numbers follow the velocity in a note's entry, only when one is not zero; older files open as before). The bridge to the Windows plugin host changed (version 5) and the host was rebuilt.
- **Velocity lane** (0.50.0): a lane under the piano roll (the **Vel** button in the piano roll header shows or hides it) with one stem per note at its start; drag a stem to set that note's velocity, drag sideways to paint the notes you pass, and when the touched note belongs to a selection of several notes, every selected note moves by the same amount. The change is applied when you lift the finger and is one undo step ("Edit velocity").
- **Routing graph in the audio engine** (0.49.9, no visible change yet): the engine can now mix tracks into **buses** (up to 16, each with gain, pan, stereo width and mute, routed to the master or to another bus; loops are refused, the order is planned so a bus is always mixed after what feeds it). Nothing in the app uses it yet (sends, bus effects and the mixer interface come with phase 4 of `docs/ROADMAP-FL-PARITY.md`); with no bus the sound is bit-for-bit what it was, checked by the offline golden renders (`native/dsptest/run_engine.sh --strict`, see `docs/testing.md`).
- **One undo / redo for the whole project** (0.49.8): **File → Undo / Redo** (the menu shows what will be undone, e.g. "Undo: Move clips") and the ↶ ↷ buttons of the piano roll now work on a single list of named actions (up to 100), not per track. Covered: notes (add, delete, move, resize, paste…), audio clips (add, import, delete, move, clone, paste, gain, pitch/speed edits), track volume / pan / stereo width / mute / solo, master volume and width, tempo, project scale, track renames and automation (points, lanes). A fader or slider drag is one step. Not undoable yet (and removing a track empties the list, since older steps may refer to it): adding or removing tracks and effects, plugin parameters, presets.
- **Versioned project files** (0.49.7): the `.zedok` file now carries a format number (2). A project saved by an older version is migrated step by step when opened (nothing is dropped, the keys the loader used to assume are written down), and the next save writes the new format; the previous file is kept in `Backup/` as always. A project saved by a *newer* version is not opened half-read: a message explains it, the project stays empty and is never saved (Save says so too), so the newer file is not overwritten. Witness projects of every format live in `app/src/test/resources/projects` with tests (migration keeps every value, is idempotent, refuses newer files).
- **Real tempo for Windows plugins** (0.49.6): VST3 and VST2 plugins now receive the project's tempo, position in quarter notes, bar start, play/stop state and time signature (4/4 for now) for every block, instead of a fixed 120 BPM, so tempo-synced delays, LFOs and arpeggiators follow the project. Measured with a test plugin that clicks on every beat of the host position: the clicks of an exported file are 0.6667 s apart at 90 BPM, 0.6 s at 100 and 0.5 s at 120. When playback is stopped the plugin is told it is stopped (it used to be told it was always playing). Only the VST2 path was measured; the VST3 path has the same change but no VST3 test plugin was available.
- **Piano roll selection fix** (0.49.5): the "one tap does nothing, a second deselects" rule now applies only when several notes are selected; with a single selected note, a tap elsewhere places a new note and selects it, as usual.
- **Piano roll selection** (0.49.4): with notes selected, one tap elsewhere does nothing and a second tap deselects all of them (it used to add a note and clear the selection at once). Every selected note now shows its two resize handles, like FL Studio, and dragging one handle resizes all the selected notes together, by the same amount (the end handle lengthens or shortens them, the start handle moves every start; none can go below zero or shorter than the grid step).
- **Fix: the play cursor froze and the sound stopped while a finger rested on the ruler (0.49.3).** A touch screen keeps reporting a finger that does not move (pressure and size changes), and each report restarted playback from the same spot, several times per second: silence and a cursor that seemed stuck. The ruler now seeks only when the finger really moves, and the same position is not sent twice in a row while playing.
- **Settings icon**: a real cog wheel (8 teeth and a hole), drawn from scratch in code (no icon taken from anywhere), instead of the sun-like glyph.
- **Preset preview note** (Settings → Plugins) now offers **C3**, C4, C5 and Off, and applies to every built-in instrument (the default stays C4).
- **Plugin manager** shows the exact location of every plugin: the full path of each Windows plugin file (selectable, long press to copy), and for the Zedok plugins that they are built into the app (and, for the piano, where its sound pack is on the phone).
- **Kit browser picker**: the list has room under its last row, so the last folders can be scrolled fully into view.
- **Zedok Sampler waveform shows what will be played**: *Reverse* flips it (the left marker is always where playing starts), *Normalize* scales it to the sample's peak, and the fades are drawn into it.
- **Edit sample…** (Sampler, Sample page): the clip editor's pitch, time (stretch, fit to the project tempo) and gain for the Sampler's sample, plus the **pitch of the sample** (detected by autocorrelation, shown as a note with cents) and **To nearest C**, which moves a sample that is not a C onto a C so the root note is right. **Use in sampler** renders the new version into the project's Samples folder ("… edited.wav") and loads it in the plugin.

- Your whole Windows environment is visible: Wine's `C:` drive, your plugins, projects and samples live in a folder you choose. Like FL Studio, every project has its own folder with everything it needs:

Plugins and installers stay shared, as does Wine's `C:` drive. Projects, samples and exports from before this layout are moved or still listed.
- Interface in US English.

## Requirements

- An **arm64** Android device, **Android 9 or newer**. A recent flagship is strongly recommended: plugins run under CPU emulation.
- About 450 MB of internal storage for the runtime and about 400 MB in the folder you choose.
- Windows `.vst3` files that you own (none are included).

## Install

1. Download the APK from the [Releases](../../releases) page and install it (allow installs from your browser or file manager).
2. Open ZedokMusic, tap **Install**, allow storage access, then choose or create a folder (not the top level of your storage).
   The first start takes about a minute and a half.
3. Either run the plugin's Windows installer: **Add → Install plugin (.exe)…**, **Import…** the `.exe`/`.msi` (it is copied to `<your folder>/Installers`), **Run** it, and use its windows with touch (**Keys** brings up the keyboard). Plugins installed to `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3` are found automatically. Or copy your `.vst3` plugins into `<your folder>/Plugins/VST3`. Plugins that keep resources in a data folder (for example u-he's `Podolski.data`) need that folder **next to the `.vst3` file**, otherwise they show a "data missing" dialog and their editor cannot open.
4. **Add → Instrument track (VST3)…**, pick a plugin, wait for the green dot, tap the pattern and write notes.

Every release APK is signed with the same key. Certificate SHA-256: see the release notes.

## How it works

```
Compose UI ── JNI ── C++ engine (Oboe) ──┐
                                          │  shared memory (events in, audio out)
                    Wine + Box64 ── zhost.exe ── your VST3 plugin
```

- `app/src/main/cpp/` — engine (`engine.cpp`), plugin bridge endpoint (`plugin_bridge.h`), WAV I/O, tar.zst extractor.
- `native/common/bridge.h` — the shared-memory protocol, used by both sides.
- `native/host/` — `zhost.exe`, the Windows-side VST3 host (cross-compiled with mingw-w64).
- `app/src/main/java/com/zedokmusic/` — UI (`ui/`), project model (`model/`), runtime installer (`wine/`), engine facade (`engine/`).

### Why `targetSdk 28` and the package name `com.zedokmusic`

Android 10+ forbids executing binaries from writable app storage for apps targeting API 29 or higher, and Wine/Box64 need exactly that.
ZedokMusic therefore targets API 28, which is **not accepted by Google Play** — releases are published here on GitHub instead.
glibc, Box64 and Wine also have their install prefix compiled in, so the app id and the `files/root` directory name are fixed
(the prefix is rewritten at build time to a string of identical length).

## Building

Needs: JDK 21, Android SDK + NDK 27, `gh` or `curl`, `unzip`, `zstd`, `perl`, and for the plugin host `mingw-w64`.

```sh
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/steinbergmedia/vst3_pluginterfaces.git build/vst3_pluginterfaces
./tools/build-rootfs.sh     # downloads Winlator 11.2 and produces app/src/main/assets/root.tar.zst (~80 MB, not in git)
./tools/build-host.sh       # builds app/src/main/assets/zhost.exe
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug
```

For a signed release, put a keystore description in `~/.zedokmusic-signing/keystore.properties`
(`storeFile`, `storePassword`, `keyAlias`, `keyPassword`) or point `ZEDOK_SIGNING_PROPS` at one, then run `./gradlew :app:assembleRelease`.
Debug builds contain adb test hooks (`DebugHooks`); release builds do not.

## Known limitations

- Plugin editors are mirrored at about 30 fps; up to three stay open at once (opening a fourth closes the oldest), and on wide screens two editors are shown side by side, with a **Full** button that gives the editor the whole area under the transport. Input: one finger = mouse, two-finger drag = mouse wheel, two-finger tap = right click, the **Keys** button opens the keyboard. (The two-finger gestures and typing are implemented but only lightly tested.)
- Each plugin host uses roughly 40% of a CPU core while playing (about 13% when idle).
- MIDI input and audio recording are implemented but only the microphone path has been tested on hardware; USB and Bluetooth MIDI devices are untested (the MIDI parser has unit tests).
- Automation curves are line, step or smooth only, and are stored per parameter in beats.
- Installers run under Wine on a 1024×768 virtual desktop; anything that needs a driver, a license service or a GPU may fail. If an installer asks to restart Windows, just close it.
- VST3 only (no VST2, no CLAP). Plugins that need a GPU, an installer-based license (iLok, etc.) or Windows-only drivers may not work.
- Heavy plugins can exceed the CPU budget on older phones. The status line shows xruns.
- Tested on one device so far (the wide layout only on the same phone with an emulated large screen). SD cards and Android 13+ storage behaviour with `targetSdk 28` are untested.
